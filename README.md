# Tulsa Finance Internships (2027 Recruiting Proxy Using 2026 Data)

Scope: undergraduate and graduate finance-track internships tied to employers with a Tulsa, OK physical presence.

## 1. Top-Ranked Tulsa Finance Internships (Overall)
1. **ONEOK – Summer Accounting/Finance Intern (Tulsa HQ)**  
   ONEOK is a Fortune 500 Tulsa-headquartered energy infrastructure company with strong corporate finance exposure and a recognizable brand in Oklahoma. Reported intern pay data is strong (commonly cited in the mid-$20s/hour range), and the HQ setting gives broad executive/network access.

2. **Williams – Summer Finance & Accounting Intern (Tulsa HQ)**  
   Williams is another Fortune 500 Tulsa HQ employer with structured finance/accounting internships and a clear corporate-finance learning environment. Public listings for 2026 proxy cycles indicate strong compensation support (hourly pay plus housing/relocation support on many postings).

3. **BOK Financial – Commercial Banking Intern (Tulsa)**  
   BOK is Tulsa’s flagship financial institution and one of the most relevant finance employers for local students targeting banking careers. Its internship-to-ACT pipeline and formal training model make it one of the strongest conversion-path opportunities in-market.

4. **BOK Financial – Wealth Management Intern (Tulsa)**  
   This role provides direct exposure to advisory, portfolio, and client service workflows in a respected regional platform. It is especially strong for students targeting private wealth, trust, and long-term relationship-management careers.

5. **Arvest Bank – Intern Program (Commercial/Risk/Wealth tracks in OK footprint including Tulsa market)**  
   Arvest’s internship is broad, paid, and frequently includes finance-adjacent banking tracks (commercial lending, risk, treasury, wealth). It offers a solid development platform with practical banking rotations for undergraduates.

6. **MidFirst Bank – Summer Officer Development Program Internship (Oklahoma market)**  
   MidFirst has a reputable structured internship and post-grad ODP path with meaningful analyst/commercial/risk exposure. While many postings center on OKC, it remains a strong Tulsa-relevant bank due to regional footprint and career transferability.

7. **Forvis Mazars – Audit/Tax Intern (Tulsa office; finance-adjacent)**  
   For students open to accounting-to-finance pathways, this is a high-quality feeder into transaction, valuation, FP&A, and corporate finance careers. Brand value and technical training are strong, even though this is not pure banking.

8. **Regent Bank – Internship / early-career Credit & Commercial Banking opportunities (Tulsa HQ)**  
   Regent provides direct local-market banking exposure in commercial and credit functions, valuable for students seeking community/regional banking careers. Program structure is less standardized than larger peers, so outcomes depend more on team placement.

## 2. Bank Internships Broken Down by Sector
### BOK Financial (Tulsa HQ)
* **Investment Banking / Capital Markets:** ACT internship rotations may include capital-markets-aligned functions depending on annual openings.
* **Commercial & Corporate Banking:** Commercial Banking Intern (10-week summer model, analyst/sales/credit exposure, pipeline to ACT full-time).
* **Wealth Management / Private Banking / Financial Planning:** Wealth Management Intern (private wealth, trust, client advisory support).
* **Risk Management / Compliance / Credit Analysis:** Credit/risk-oriented analyst development via ACT and related bank functions.
* **Retail Banking & Operations:** Retail/operations exposure through broader internship rotation and branch-support functions.

### Arvest Bank (Tulsa market presence)
* **Investment Banking / Capital Markets:** Limited direct IB; treasury/corporate-finance style projects appear in some intern cohorts.
* **Commercial & Corporate Banking:** Commercial lending and treasury management intern placements.
* **Wealth Management / Private Banking / Financial Planning:** Wealth-management internship opportunities within Arvest’s advisory businesses.
* **Risk Management / Compliance / Credit Analysis:** Risk-management and credit support internships in enterprise and line-of-business teams.
* **Retail Banking & Operations:** Community-banking operations and branch-oriented intern roles across its regional footprint.

### MidFirst Bank (regional presence in Tulsa)
* **Investment Banking / Capital Markets:** Not a primary intern focus; occasional corporate-finance exposure through structured projects.
* **Commercial & Corporate Banking:** ODP/Summer internships with commercial banking and business-client analysis assignments.
* **Wealth Management / Private Banking / Financial Planning:** Private banking and relationship-management exposure via ODP track options.
* **Risk Management / Compliance / Credit Analysis:** Formal risk/compliance and financial-analysis components in internship casework.
* **Retail Banking & Operations:** Bank operations and process exposure through rotations and team projects.

### Regent Bank (Tulsa HQ)
* **Investment Banking / Capital Markets:** No consistent dedicated IB/capital-markets internship track identified.
* **Commercial & Corporate Banking:** Commercial lending and relationship-banking oriented early-career opportunities.
* **Wealth Management / Private Banking / Financial Planning:** Limited dedicated intern postings; occasional portfolio/client roles can overlap.
* **Risk Management / Compliance / Credit Analysis:** Credit analyst and risk/compliance-oriented roles are the clearest finance path.
* **Retail Banking & Operations:** Operations, loan documentation, and process roles appear regularly.
